# Falling Islets - Haiku Building Game

A colorful HTML5 video game where players control a paddle at the bottom of the screen to catch falling islets (colored text blocks) representing different parts of speech, with the objective of building haikus (5-7-5 syllable poems).

## How to Play

- Use the **left** and **right** arrow keys or **A/D** keys to move your paddle
- Press **SPACE** to start the game
- Press **SPACE** or **P** to pause/resume the game
- Click the **🔊** button to toggle sound effects on/off
- **Catch falling islets to build haikus!** Collect words to create 5-7-5 syllable poems
- Multiple islets fall simultaneously at different speeds
- Each islet displays a word representing a different part of speech with color coding:
  - 🔴 **Red** = Verbs
  - 🟣 **Purple** = Nouns  
  - 🟡 **Yellow** = Adjectives
  - 🟢 **Green** = Adverbs
  - 🟠 **Orange** = Articles
  - ⚪ **White** = Special Power Islets (POW!)
  - 🔵 **Light Blue** = Bomb Islets (BOOM!)

## Special Features

### Special Power Islets
- **White POW! islets**: Activate special powers when caught
- **Light Blue BOOM! islets**: Dangerous bombs that remove your last word when caught

### Special Powers
- **Wider Paddle**: Makes your paddle wider for easier catching
- **Narrower Paddle**: Makes your paddle narrower for more challenge
- **Inverted Controls**: Reverses left/right movement controls
- **Ultra Speed**: Increases paddle movement speed
- **Exploding Islets**: All regular islets become dangerous and remove your last word
- **Upside Down**: Flips the entire screen upside down for 5 seconds

### Visual Effects
- **Blinking animations**: Special islets (POW! and BOOM!) blink to draw attention
- **Exploding islets power**: All regular islets blink light blue and show "BOOM!" when the power is active
- **Screen effects**: Special powers create visual feedback with border animations
- **Animated background**: Twinkling stars create a dynamic backdrop
- **Paddle design**: Clean underscore-based paddle design

## Game Mechanics

### Haiku Building
- **5-7-5 Structure**: First line has exactly 5 syllables, second line has 7, third line has 5
- **Real-time display**: Current haiku is shown in the center of the screen
- **Word removal**: Bombs and exploding islets remove your last collected word
- **Automatic line breaks**: Words automatically move to the next line when syllable limits are reached

### Movement System
- **Acceleration**: Hold movement keys longer to move faster
- **Smooth controls**: Responsive paddle movement with momentum
- **Collision detection**: Precise hit detection for catching islets

### Scoring System
- **Base points**: Earn points for each word caught
- **Haiku completion**: 100 bonus points for completing a haiku
- **Progressive difficulty**: Game speed increases over time
- **Score tracking**: Current score and haiku progress displayed

## Technical Details

- **Built with**: HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript
- **No external dependencies**: Pure vanilla JavaScript
- **Responsive design**: Works on different screen sizes
- **Monospace font**: Consistent text rendering for islets
- **Sound effects**: Audio feedback for game actions
- **Local storage**: Game state persistence

## Game Rules

1. **Catch words**: Use your paddle to catch falling word islets
2. **Build haikus**: Collect words to create 5-7-5 syllable poems
3. **Avoid bombs**: Light blue BOOM! islets will remove your last word
4. **Use special powers**: White POW! islets give you temporary abilities
5. **Complete haikus**: Finish poems to earn bonus points and continue playing
6. **Don't let words fall**: Missed words are lost forever

## Controls

- **Arrow Keys** or **A/D**: Move paddle left/right
- **SPACE**: Start game / Pause/Resume
- **P**: Pause/Resume (alternative)
- **Mouse**: Click sound toggle button

## Tips for Success

- **Plan your haiku**: Think about syllable counts before catching words
- **Use special powers wisely**: Some powers help, others add challenge
- **Watch for bombs**: Light blue islets are dangerous!
- **Build momentum**: Hold movement keys for faster paddle speed
- **Practice**: The more you play, the better you'll get at haiku building

Enjoy the game and improve your parts of speech recognition and haiku writing skills!
